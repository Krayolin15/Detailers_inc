    —Questions

    Before you book.

    Do you provide mobile detailing?

    Yes. Detailers Inc. is a mobile service: we come to you and bring the whole process to your door.

    What areas do you service?

    We are based in Mount Edgecombe, Durban, and travel to our customers. Send us your suburb on WhatsApp and we will confirm whether we can reach you.

    What services are available?

    Four: Full Maintenance Wash, Vehicle Detailing, Vehicle Polishing and Ceramic Coating. Each has one or more packages, listed in the services section.

    How long does an appointment take?

    It depends on the package. A Full Maintenance Wash takes 45 – 60 minutes. An Interior Detail takes 3 – 4 hours. Polishing takes 4 – 8 hours. Full details and ceramic coatings take between one and three days.

    Do prices vary by vehicle size?

    Yes. Each package is priced for three sizes: Sedan / Hatch, Mini SUV / Crossover and Large SUV / 4x4. The booking form shows the listed price for your choice, and we confirm the final quote when we reply.

    How do I book?

    Use the booking form, which opens WhatsApp with your details already written, or message or call us directly on +27 84 877 2847. We are available Monday to Saturday, 8AM – 6PM.
